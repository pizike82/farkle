How to play

Bank 10,000 to win

Roll six dice. Set aside a scoring set, then roll the rest or bank the turn into your total. A roll with nothing scoring is a Farkle — that turn’s points are gone.

If every remaining die scores, you have hot dice and may roll all six again. First to 10,000 wins.

Scoring

  • Single 1 = 100  ·  Single 5 = 50
  • Three 1s = 1,000; three of any other face = that face × 100
  • Each extra die of that kind doubles the three-of-a-kind score
  • Straight (1–6) = 1,500
  • Three pairs = 1,500
  • Two triplets = 2,500
  • Four of a kind + a pair = 1,500

Order sorts the tray. Pause freezes the clock. Abandon and New game are in the gear menu once you’re playing.
